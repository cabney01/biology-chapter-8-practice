  • Chromosomes, chromatin, genes, chromatids

  • Meiosis I and II; crossing over

8B • Inheritance

  • Mendel's genetic principles
  • Alleles, genotype, phenotype
  • Monohybrid and dihybrid Punnett squares
  • Dominance patterns, pedigrees, sex-linked traits

8C • Gene Expression

  • Promoters, enhancers, silencers
  • Introns, exons, transcription
  • Environmental influences on genes
  • Cell differentiation and stem cells
Remember: Meiosis creates genetically varied gametes, inherited alleles influence traits, and gene regulation determines which genetic instructions cells use.
